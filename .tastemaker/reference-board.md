# Reference board — Natalie Gaida

Status: viewed sources (live brand site + in-repo brand guide). Adjacent therapy sites searched 2026-09-14 for category context; not adopted as pixel sources.

## Design read
- Surface: public couples-therapy marketing site
- Audience: thoughtful partners in Massachusetts, often tired of the same fight, not in acute crisis theater
- Visitor mode: Persuade + Read
- Visual lane: warm editorial (not clinical, not spa-wellness kitsch)
- Dials: variance 4 · motion 3 · density 3 · art direction 7

## Anti-references
- Framer “Serein” and similar therapy marketplace templates (luxury template sameness)
- Purple/indigo SaaS gradients, glassmorphism, KPI hero strips
- Stock therapy-office handshakes, over-posed romance, harsh flash
- UI/UX Pro Max default “Liquid Glass” + Montserrat recommendation for this brief
- Cream + terracotta Anthropic-default look without this practice’s photography and voice

## Direction contract
- Thesis: a calm room for an honest conversation
- First viewport: brand · one promise · one short line · one Book CTA · one relational photo
- System: locked blush/cocoa/terracotta + dual Garamond + tonal long-scroll
- Risk: therapy cliché or clinical coldness
