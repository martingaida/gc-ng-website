01 Foundation

Help couples name the pattern, then take a clear next step

The site feels sophisticated without becoming clinical. Warmth lowers the barrier; hierarchy clarifies the path; restraint protects trust.

Principle
Expression
Core promise
Recognize the pattern, rebuild connection, act with clarity.
Emotional register
Grounded, intimate, hopeful, unhurried. Never sentimental or vague.
Voice
Direct and conversational. Name the tension, normalize the experience, offer a specific next step.
Audience
Thoughtful partners ready to do real work, not consume generic relationship tips.
Design character
Dual Garamond, warm tonal fields, softly cropped photography, sparse line gestures.

Three filters before shipping a page

  • Does it feel human and specific rather than therapeutic by template?
  • Can a stressed visitor understand the message and next step in seconds?
  • Is the design quietly confident, with enough space to breathe?

02 Color contract

Warm neutrals with one verified action color

Blush and white carry the page. Cocoa carries the words. Terracotta accents. Dark terracotta is the only fill for white CTA labels.

Blush #EDD9CF Primary soft background
Cocoa #514139 Text and dark anchor
Action #A16657 CTA fill with white text
Terracotta #C1806C Accent, underline, large type
Sand #C8A47E Field only, not body copy
White / Pale #FFF / #F8F3F0 Relief and nested panels

Hard rules. White text only on Action #A16657 (4.61:1). Never use white on #C1806C at normal size. Never set body copy on Sand (cocoa on sand is 4.19:1). Muted text is #74645C, not #88766D.

08 Motifs

Use a line to imply reconnection

Organic underlines, continuous paths, overlapping circles, and the couple-heart mark. Sparse on purpose.

Preserve marks byte-for-byte. Do not recolor or replace without an explicit rebrand.

09 Accessibility

Warmth with stronger interaction states

Contrast is part of the brand contract, not polish after the fact.

Cocoa on blush 7.13:1 AAA body
Cocoa on white 9.70:1 AAA body
White on action 4.61:1 AA body · required CTA
Muted on white 5.65:1 AA secondary text
White on terracotta 3.20:1 Large text only · never normal CTA
Cocoa on sand 4.19:1 Large text only · no body copy
  • Visible 2–3 px focus outline with strong adjacent contrast.
  • Tap targets at least 44×44 CSS pixels.
  • Descriptive alt for meaningful photos; empty alt for decorative motifs.
  • Respect prefers-reduced-motion.
  • One logical heading order; no skip from h1 to h3 for style.
  • Do not rely on color alone for state.

10 Guardrails

Protect the warmth through restraint

Consistency comes from repeating a small number of confident choices.

Do
Don't
Use specific, emotionally intelligent language.
Use vague wellness slogans or therapy clichés.
Pair generous space with one clear next step.
Fill gaps with badges, KPI strips, or decoration.
Use candid photography with relational cues.
Use generic offices or over-posed romance.
Keep one primary CTA per content zone.
Present several equal-priority buttons together.
Use organic motifs as a quiet accent.
Scatter lines, hearts, and infinity symbols.
Stay on Cormorant + Garamond.
Add Inter, Montserrat, purple gradients, or glassmorphism.

Page build checklist

  • One visitor tension, one outcome, one primary action.
  • Specific H1, supporting line, one CTA, one relational image.
  • Name the pattern before leading with credentials.
  • Repeat the CTA only after meaningful decision points.

Launch checks

  • Test 320, 768, 1024, and 1440 px widths.
  • Verify 200% zoom, keyboard use, and reduced motion.
  • Check contrast pairings against the lock matrix.
  • Confirm heading order, alt text, and target size.
